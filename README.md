# SenzoTRF — Multi-Agentic Trading Research Framework

**SenzoTRF** is an multi-agentic trading research framework designed to analyze stocks through parallel specialist analysts, rigorous debate, and an advisory panel. By dividing market research into specialized sub-tasks and synthesizing multiple viewpoints, Senzo generates actionable, balanced, and data-backed trading verdicts.

> **Inspiration & Acknowledgments:**  
> Senzo takes inspiration from the work of **[Tauric Research's TradingAgents](https://github.com/TauricResearch/TradingAgents)**. If you are interested in multi-agent trading systems, check out their repository and research!
